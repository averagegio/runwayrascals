STATUSES (single value per row; overwrite, do not stack):
NEW              — imported, not yet first-touched
QUEUED           — first-touch queued for a weekday send slot
SENT_1           — first-touch sent
SENT_2           — day-6 follow-up sent
SENT_3           — day-13 breakup sent
OOO              — auto-reply detected; hold sequence
BOUNCE           — undeliverable; DNC this address
REPLIED_MEETING  — human asked to talk / proposed time
REPLIED_REFERRAL — human pointed to another person / offered intro
REPLIED_PASS     — explicit or implied no
REPLIED_OTHER    — human reply that is not meeting / referral / pass (founder reads)
EXHAUSTED        — 3 touches complete, no reply
DNC              — do not contact (PASS, bounce, unsubscribe, legal, hostile)
HOLD             — founder paused (e.g. warm intro incoming)

HOW TO CLASSIFY REPLIES (first matching rule wins; case-insensitive):

bounce
  From looks like Mailer-Daemon / postmaster / MAILER-DAEMON
  OR subject/body contains: undeliverable, delivery status notification, 550, 5.1.1, user unknown, mailbox not found, address rejected
  Action: status=BOUNCE, DNC=yes, stop sequence, do not retry a guessed address.

ooo
  Body looks like an auto-reply AND contains: out of office, out-of-office, OOO, away until, on leave, automatic reply, vacation
  AND does not also contain a clear PASS / not a fit
  Action: status=OOO. Parse a return date if present; set next_touch_at to the next weekday on or after return+1. Do not increment touches. Do not send while OOO. If no date, hold 10 weekdays then resume remaining touches.

pass
  Exact token PASS as its own reply
  OR body/subject contains: pass, not a fit, not investing, too early, unsubscribe, remove me, stop emailing, no longer investing, not in our thesis, decline
  Action: status=REPLIED_PASS, DNC=yes, stop sequence. Do not argue.

meeting
  Human (not auto-reply) AND any of: let's chat, let's talk, intro call, hop on, grab time, calendar, Calendly, here's my availability, next week works, I'm free, send times, 15 min, 20 min, happy to meet, book a slot
  OR they propose a clock time / date
  Action: status=REPLIED_MEETING, stop sequence, notify founder. Do not auto-send a calendar hold.

referral
  Human AND any of: intro you to, connecting you, cc'ing, looping in, talk to [Name], better sent to, our games partner, forward this to, reach out to
  Action: status=REPLIED_REFERRAL, stop sequence on THIS address, notify founder. New person becomes a NEW row (do not first-touch them the same day as the referral reply).

else
  status=REPLIED_OTHER, stop sequence, notify founder to read.
