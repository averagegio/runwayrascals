ARR model

Commerce + membership,
not ads-first.

Marketing strategy

Culture first.
Performance second.

  • Content engine: 15s runway fails / fits UGC kit
  • Creator seeding: 50–100 micro fashion + gaming creators
  • Drop calendar: aligned to real Fashion Weeks
  • Community: Discord / IG close friends for pilots
  • Paid: Meta + TikTok after organic creative wins
  • SEO / ASO: “fashion game” / runway keywords
  • PR: fashion-tech + indie game press
  • Partners: boutiques, schools, showrooms

Grow MAU

Monthly active users — levers

  • Acquisition: waitlist → open cohorts; creator codes; shareable finish cards.
  • Activation: <60s to first run; default free street pack; onboarding look.
  • Retention: weekly Fashion Week events; new city / gait / set cadence.
  • Referral: invite for exclusive RR mark / early drop access.

Internal targets (planning): 10k MAU post-pilot · 100k MAU Year 1 · 500k MAU Year 2 — subject to capital & live-ops hire.

Brand outreach

Labels as levels.
Drops as seasons.

  • Outbound to emerging designers first (fast legal, high culture signal).
  • Capsule collabs: in-game set + limited real-world merch or sample.
  • Showroom / fashion-week activations: playable booths, QR to waitlist.
  • Clear licensing template: revenue share on digital SKU + brand-safe guidelines.

Current boutique uses inspired designer-named SKUs for product vision — licensed partnerships required for commercial brand use.

IP rights

Own the universe.
License the logos.

  • Company IP: RR marks, game code, characters, original garments, UX, soundtrack briefs.
  • Trademarks: file “Rascal Runways” + RR device marks (US + key markets).
  • 3rd-party brands: only ship licensed or wholly original designs commercially.
  • UGC / scans: ToS — user grants license to uploaded looks; no claim on their personal likeness beyond game use.
  • Employees / contractors: work-for-hire + IP assignment on all art & code.

Exit strategy

Several doors — build for options.

  • Strategic (gaming): publisher / platform acquiring live-service + fashion audience.
  • Strategic (fashion / luxury): house or conglomerate buying interactive brand channel.
  • Strategic (social / creators): company buying playable commerce graph.
  • Financial: growth equity / late VC if category leader in fashion play.
  • Timing: optimize for Seed→A proof; exit conversations when multi-brand seasons + retention prove the loop (often Year 4–7).
